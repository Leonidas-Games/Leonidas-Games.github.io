Parking Bonanza

Run a busy 3D car park. Cars roll in through the boom gate, and every one needs a bay of its own colour. Drivers take the first free bay in the row you pick, waiting bays are few, and traffic lights, ambulances, motorbikes, zebra crossings and caravans keep you thinking. Sometimes the power even cuts out, and you park from memory. 200 levels, eight car parks and a new daily puzzle every day.
