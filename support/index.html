Parking Bonanza

How do I play?

Tap a row of bays to send the car at the gate into it. The driver takes the first free bay they reach, and that bay's colour must match the car. If no bay is ready, park the car in a waiting bay for now. You don't have to wait for cars to finish driving: tap as fast as you like. Every car park adds something new, from traffic lights to caravans, and the game explains it the first time it appears.

The car park went dark!

That's a power cut. From level 150, the lights sometimes go out for one move. You get a 3-second warning, so remember where the bays are, then park the car at the gate from memory. The lights come back straight after.

I'm stuck on a level

Tap Hint to see the best next move. You get 3 free hints, then each one is a short video. You can also undo a move or rewind to the last point where the level could still be won (each after a short video), or restart the level for free. Every level can be solved.

How do stars work?

Three stars for finishing in the fewest possible moves, two for close to it, and one for finishing. Stars unlock paint jobs for your cars in the Garage.

What's the daily car park?

A new puzzle every day, which opens after level 8. Finish it on days in a row to build a streak, and share your result with friends.

Will I lose my progress?

Your progress is saved on your phone. It stays when you update the game, but it's erased if you delete the game. There's no account, so progress can't move between devices.

Why are there ads?

Ads keep the game free. You choose when to watch a video for a reward, and a full-screen ad sometimes appears between levels, never in the middle of one. See our privacy policy for how ads use information, and how to change your choices.
