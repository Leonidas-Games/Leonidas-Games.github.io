Privacy policy

Effective 1 November 2026

This policy covers the mobile games made by Leonidas Games, including Parking Puzzle. In short: we don't collect anything about you ourselves, there are no accounts, and your progress stays on your phone. The games show ads from Google, and Google collects some information to do that. This page explains what, and how you can control it.

What we collect

Nothing. Our games have no sign-up or accounts, and we don't run our own servers or analytics. Your progress, stars, streaks and settings are saved only on your device. They're never sent to us, and they're deleted if you delete the game.

Ads from Google AdMob

Our games are free because they show ads, provided by Google AdMob. You choose when to watch rewarded videos (for a hint, an undo or an extra waiting bay), and a full-screen ad sometimes appears between levels.

To show and measure ads, Google's software in the game may collect:

  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (for example, your country or city).
  • Device identifiers: your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking) and other identifiers tied to the app or developer.
  • Advertising data, such as which ads you've seen and whether you tapped them.
  • How you interact with the game, such as app launches, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostic data, such as crash logs and performance information (for example, app launch time).

Google uses this information to show ads, to measure how ads perform, to prevent fraud, for analytics, and to improve its products. Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or other identifiers to do this. You can read how Google uses this information at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, and in Google's privacy policy.

We ask Google to show only ads suitable for most audiences.

Your choices

  • Tracking on iPhone: the game asks whether it may use your advertising identifier. If you say no, you'll still see ads, but they won't be based on your activity in other apps. You can change this any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • In the UK, EU and Switzerland: the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice any time in the game under Settings › Privacy choices.
  • Ad personalisation: you can manage how Google personalises ads at adssettings.google.com, and reset your device's advertising identifier in your phone's settings.
  • Your progress: you can erase it in the game under Settings › Reset progress, or by deleting the game.

Children

Our games are suitable for everyone, but they aren't made for or aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how our games handle information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
